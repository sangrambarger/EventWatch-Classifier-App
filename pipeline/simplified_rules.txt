3. Executive Council Judge Role:
   - Synthesizes the debate between the False Negative Guardian and False Positive Controller into a single final verdict: "Impactful" vs "Not Impactful".

   - Produces a concise 1-2 sentence evidence-based Rationale citing specific entities, locations, operational downtime, or exclusion grounds.

1. CORE DECISION DEFINITIONS: IMPACTFUL VS NOT IMPACTFUL
================================================================================
- "Impactful":
  * Meaning: Reasonable evidence that an operational, physical, logistical, financial, regulatory, environmental, cyber, or labor risk exists that could affect industrial manufacturing, component supply, or customer supply chains.
  * Early Warning Doctrine: EventWatch reports potential risk to provide early warning before consequences manifest ("EventWatch reports risk, not history"). DO NOT require confirmed production shutdown or supplier public admission.
  * Enforce Prohibited Assumptions (PA-001, PA-002, PA-004, PA-028):
    - PA-001 / PA-002: Absence of evidence is NOT evidence of absence. Lack of immediate supplier comment or unquantified damage does NOT make an event Not Impactful.
    - PA-004: Absence of an explicit production halt statement does NOT mean operations are unaffected.
    - PA-028: Uncertainty mandates conservative retention. If an event presents plausible operational downtime or supply delay, classify as "Impactful".

- "Not Impactful":
  * Meaning: The event has zero supply chain relevance, is low-value/bad-article noise, or has completely concluded with zero operational disruption and zero ongoing consequences.
  * Must strictly satisfy the Tripartite Exclusion Rule or the Bad Article Taxonomy.
  * Never classify as Not Impactful merely because the company name is unfamiliar or damage details are pending.

In Resilinc's architecture, "Mapped Partners" are registered suppliers, vendor facilities, and OEM manufacturing sites in customer supply chain graphs. In unlinked bulk feeds, apply this mandatory rule:
- Tier-1 OEMs & Fortune 500 Giants (Automatic Positive Reporting Bias):

  * Any plant fire, operational halt, strike, cyber attack, regulatory action, executive fraud, financial distress, or major litigation involving these entities is AUTOMATICALLY IMPACTFUL.
- Unmapped Industrial Suppliers ("Assume Sub-Tier or T1 Yet to Be Mapped"):

If it operates in core industrial verticals (Semiconductors, Electronics, Industrial Automation, Precision Machining, Automotive Components, Specialty Chemicals, Medical Devices, Aerospace Parts, Mining/Metals, Logistics/Warehousing), ASSUME IT IS A CRITICAL TIER-2 OR TIER-3 SUPPLIER (or a T1 yet to be mapped).

- Common Cross-Industry Services (Group B Verticals):

Disruptions to these entities are AUTOMATICALLY IMPACTFUL.

3. EXCLUSION OF EVENTS ALREADY "OVER" WITH ZERO DISRUPTION (TRIPARTITE RULE)
================================================================================
An event may be classified as "Not Impactful" under the "Already Over" rule IF AND ONLY IF ALL THREE of the following conditions are simultaneously met:
1. Full Resolution Prior to Reporting: The trigger incident was brief, immediately contained, averted, or completely concluded prior to the news release.
   Examples:
   - Airport: Flight delays cleared within hours; passenger delays only; cargo operations experienced zero delay.
   - Power Outage: Non-semiconductor grid outage restored in <6 hours with no industrial plant impact.
   - Chemical Spill: Minor spill fully contained in secondary bunds/berms; zero toxic plume; zero evacuations; zero road closures.
   - Labor Disruption: Strike ballot averted via ratified labor agreement; strike called off before any work stoppage occurred.
   - Weather: Weather advisory or storm watch expired without touchdown, damage, or flooding.
   - Legal/Regulatory: Investigation dropped or dismissed without sanction, fine, or injunction.
2. Zero Realized or Ongoing Operational Disruption: Zero facility closures, zero manufacturing downtime, zero damaged tooling, zero lost inventory, zero freight backlogs, and zero ongoing regulatory shutdown orders.
3. Non-Actionable Historical Narrative: The story is purely a historical retrospective, memorial anniversary, past case study, or stale news (>48 hours old) recounting an event long resolved with no active developments.

================================================================================
4. MANDATORY ZERO-HOUR EXCEPTIONS (NEVER EXCLUDE EVEN IF "OVER")
================================================================================
The following events MUST ALWAYS be classified as IMPACTFUL, regardless of claims that they are "small", "contained", or "already over":
- Exception 1: PA-007 / PA-008 Factory & Warehouse Fires:
  * ALL fires and explosions at manufacturing plants, fabs, industrial warehouses, distribution centers, and refineries are ALWAYS IMPACTFUL AT ZERO HOURS.
  * Extinguishment does NOT mean the event is over. Soot contamination, electrical grid repairs, water damage from fire hoses/sprinklers, structural safety inspections, cleanroom recalibration, and regulatory work-stop orders routinely cause days or weeks of disruption.
- Exception 2: Semiconductor Fab & Cleanroom Power Outages / Voltage Sags:
  * Power interruptions of even milliseconds/seconds at semiconductor wafer fabs, cleanrooms, or precision chemical synthesis facilities cause immediate tool trips, wafer batch scrap ($5M–$30M per batch), and days of recalibration downtime. The standard 6-hour power outage threshold DOES NOT APPLY to semiconductor fabs. ALL fab power outages and voltage sags of ANY duration are ALWAYS IMPACTFUL AT ZERO HOURS.
- Exception 3: Hazmat / Toxic Releases with Evacuation:
  * Any chemical or gas release that triggered worker evacuations, shelter-in-place orders, highway closures, or environmental regulator investigations is ALWAYS IMPACTFUL AT ZERO HOURS, regardless of rapid containment.

================================================================================
5. BAD ARTICLE TAXONOMY & NON-EVENTS (14 CATEGORIES — ALWAYS NOT IMPACTFUL)
================================================================================
Immediately classify content falling under any of the 14 Bad Article categories as NOT IMPACTFUL:
1. civilian_incident_only: Domestic car crashes on local roads, residential house/apartment fires, local crimes, muggings, non-industrial park accidents with no commercial freight or manufacturing impact.
2. law_firm_advertisement: Press releases by plaintiff law firms (e.g., Rosen Law, Robbins Geller, Pomerantz) announcing investigations or shareholder loss solicitations.
3. lawsuit_solicitation: Advertisements soliciting consumers or workers who used a product to join class actions or mass torts.
4. historical_recap: Retrospective reviews, historical anniversary articles ("10 years since Hurricane Sandy", "Remembering Fukushima"), past case studies with zero active disruption.
5. sports_story: Athletic games, soccer/football scores, player contract disputes, Olympic news, stadium renovations.
6. celebrity_story: Entertainment gossip, red carpet events, actor divorces, celebrity lifestyle posts, royal family news.
7. promotional_pr: Sponsored vendor puff pieces, corporate culture awards ("Best place to work"), minor marketing announcements, trade show attendance notices.
8. generic_explainer: Educational glossaries, generic background articles ("What is a semiconductor?", "How cargo ships navigate canals") lacking contemporaneous disruption.
9. duplicate_rewrite_no_new_signal: Syndicated re-posts or automated rewrites providing no new facts, updates, or resolution details.
10. opinion_only: Op-eds, political commentary, macroeconomic punditry, theoretical market musings without concrete company or facility events.
11. no_operational_detail: Vague headline fragments ("Company announces update", "Executive attends meeting in Brussels") lacking specific facilities, products, or disruption signals.
12. no_disruption_signal: Normal thriving operations, record quarterly vehicle deliveries, safety milestones, routine scheduled off-hours maintenance without extraordinary stoppage.
13. unrelated_topic_noise: Municipal zoning debates, school board elections, zoo updates, space trivia, mild non-threatening weather forecasts.
14. very_thin_summary: Automated ticker fragments, single incomplete sentences, broken scraping snippets lacking context or nouns.
